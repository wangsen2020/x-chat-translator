XTrans – X (Twitter) Translator for Posts, Replies & DMs

Translate X posts, articles, replies and DMs. Read bilingually, preview drafts, and cache article blocks as you scroll.

Translate posts, replies, quote-post comments and DMs on X. Read bilingually and preview translations before sending.

Keep your reply or quote comment in the original editor and preview its translation below. Optional interception sends the translation.

Skip posts and replies already in your reading language. Reply and quote drafts are skipped only if they already match the target language.

Assign Google Translate, Gemini API (your own key), or local AI through AgentRelay independently to posts, replies, incoming DMs, and outgoing DMs.

Privacy and limits: text is sent to the selected translation service. Gemini requires your own API key; AgentRelay must be installed and running separately. Providers may retain text in conversation history. See the privacy policy. Image text and video subtitles are not translated. Check X before retrying an unknown sending result.

Articles translate one visible text block at a time, only in article details. Unseen blocks are not collected in advance. The separate Article category reuses a bounded local cache when you scroll back or reopen an article; matching text, target language and engine settings reuse the result. Article translation can choose its own engine and initially inherits your post engine.
